# RenewIQ

RenewIQ is a personal subscription tracker. See what your plans cost each month, know which renewals are coming up, and keep a record of what you paid. All amounts are shown in Indian rupees (INR).

## Spending dashboard

- See your monthly spending, projected annual cost, active plan count, and the number of renewals due this week.
- Review upcoming payments for the next 30 days.
- Compare spending by category. RenewIQ converts weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly charges to monthly equivalents so you can compare plans on the same basis.

## Subscription management

- Add, edit, or delete a subscription with its name, category, price, billing cycle, next renewal date, and optional notes.
- Choose a suggested category or enter your own. Search by name or category and filter by active, paused, or cancelled status.
- After you pay a provider, select **Paid** on an active plan and confirm the amount. RenewIQ records the payment and advances the next renewal date by one billing cycle.

## Payment history

Browse recorded payments newest first. Each entry preserves the plan name, category, billing cycle, amount, renewal date covered, and time recorded, even if you later edit or delete the plan. You can delete an individual payment record while keeping the current renewal schedule.

## Renewal reminders

- **In-app alerts** list active plans due today or in the next seven days, ordered by renewal date. The notification bell updates when you change a plan or record a payment.
- **Email reminders** can notify you 7, 3, and 1 day before renewal. Adding an active plan due within the seven-day window also triggers an immediate reminder. Email delivery requires SMTP to be enabled and configured.

## AI assistant

With an AI provider configured, you can ask questions about your account, explore spending, and look up terms in your subscription documents.

| What you can do | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Ask about tracked plans and renewal dates | “Which subscriptions renew in the next 30 days?” |
| Chart spending by category or compare individual plans | “Chart my monthly spending by category.” |
| Visualize upcoming renewals or project costs at current rates | “What would my active plans cost over six months?” |
| Request a change to a tracked plan’s status and confirm it before it takes effect | “Mark my music subscription as paused.” |
| Find policy details in an attached document and inspect the retrieved passages | “How much notice does StreamBox require for cancellation?” |

- **Chart details.** Inspect exact values in a table or export them as CSV. Saved charts retain the values from the time you asked.
- **Document library.** Upload plain text or Markdown files, choose which documents a chat can search, and detach, reuse, or delete them. Try the [sample subscription policy](examples/demo-policy.md).
- **Saved chats.** Start separate conversations, reopen a chat for follow-up questions, and rename or delete it. Messages, charts, and retrieved references stay together.

## Account and preferences

Sign up with your name, email, and password to keep your subscriptions, payments, documents, and chats under your own account. The interface adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops. Settings let you switch between light and dark themes and toggle interface sounds.
