## Fictional StreamNest cancellation policy

StreamNest cancellation must be requested at least seven days before its next renewal. Access continues until the current paid period ends. StreamNest does not refund a partially used month. These rules apply only to the fictional StreamNest plan in this document.

## Fictional WorkFlow Pro downgrade option

WorkFlow Pro can be downgraded from INR 499 per month to a Starter plan at INR 299 per month. The monthly reduction would be INR 200, and the annual reduction would be INR 2,400 if maintained for twelve months. No downgrade has actually been performed.
